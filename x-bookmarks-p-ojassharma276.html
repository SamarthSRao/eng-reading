  • 6 Dec 2025
    Today my friends and I were discussing ACID properties and the CAP theorem.

    CAP says a system can’t provide Consistency, Availability, and Partition Tolerance all at once.
    But a good database should still follow ACID. So we ended up arguing whether one of them is actually wrong.

    Turns out ,ACID and CAP don’t contradict each other. ACID ensures reliable transactions inside a database, while CAP talks about trade-offs in distributed systems between Consistency, Availability, and Partition Tolerance. Different layers, different problems.
