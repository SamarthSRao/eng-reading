Coding exercises

5 hands-on exercises, basic to advanced. Predict the number first, then build, then measure.

  • Ex 1 · Basic · Bandit testbed
    Task: In Python, compare epsilon-greedy (0.01, 0.1) and UCB on a 10-armed testbed over 2,000 runs.
    Verify: Average-reward and %-optimal-action curves have the same shape as Sutton & Barto's Figure 2.2. Source: Sutton & Barto, Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction
  • Ex 2 · Basic · Tabular Q-learning
    Task: Train Q-learning on Gymnasium FrozenLake and Taxi-v3.
    Verify: Report success rate over 1,000 eval episodes and a learning curve; tune epsilon decay and show its effect. Source: Gymnasium: Frozen Lake
  • Ex 3 · Intermediate · Policy iteration vs value iteration
    Task: Implement both on your own gridworld environment.
    Verify: Both reach the same optimal policy; compare number of sweeps; check the 4x4 values against the book's example. Source: Sutton & Barto, Chapter 4: Dynamic Programming
  • Ex 4 · Intermediate · DQN on CartPole
    Task: Implement DQN with a replay buffer and target network in PyTorch.
    Verify: Mean return ≥ 195 over 100 episodes; ablate the replay buffer and the target network and show the instability. Source: Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning
  • Ex 5 · Advanced · PPO with GAE
    Task: Implement PPO-clip with GAE and train it on CartPole or LunarLander.
    Verify: Learning curves over 5 seeds (mean and spread); ablate the clip range and advantage normalisation. Source: Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms
