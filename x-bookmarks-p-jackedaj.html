    "how to learn first principles thinking?"
    "suggest me a video"
    "how you learnt it?"

    listen, first principles thinking is nothing but the ability to "asking questions".

    in this you simply allow your intuition to come up with a questions that it doesn't have the answers of.

    the key is "distractions".

    you can't ask great questions if you're checking if you crush has viewed story of you playing guitar on insta or not every 20 mins.

    intuition works in a flow, and flow is achieved when you're not distracted for a particular period of time.

    for me it's 3-4 hrs.

    - just open a doc
    - write the problem statement
    - stare at the screen
    - repeat that statement again and again in ur mind
    - stare at the screen
    - till your intuition is focus on the problem
    - and start asking questions
    - don't judge the stupid questions
    - they're the prerequisite
    - check ur crush's vies in the evening.
    - congrats!
