  • 21 Jul 2026
    Specialize. Specialize. Specialize.
    Pick one high-demand skill and build your whole resume centered around it.

    You can easily compete with average senior devs this way.

    I usually don’t suggest being a “frameworker” but if you think you don’t have time for open source, DSA, etc. and need a job urgently, then you don’t really have many options.

    Pick some framework, make a couple of serious projects, go through common interview questions related to that framework and apply to dozens of startups/agencies.

    Once you have a job, then try to upskill and switch for a better salary.
    Links: Quoted post: twitter.com/Saffron__Archer/status/2079601007580082296
