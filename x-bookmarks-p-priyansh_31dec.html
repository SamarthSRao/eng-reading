  • 15 Nov 2025
    A common question I get these days from students is "should I solve the CP-31 sheet or CSES problemset?"

    If you're trying to get better at a particular standard topic like dynamic programming, trees or range queries, you should go for CSES. This way you solve some really standard must-solve problems in a topic before you can pick up the harder and non-obvious ones.

    If you're looking to get introduced to ideas common in a particular rating on Codeforces, you should go for CP-31 sheet. This way you can learn a lot more useful concepts by solving just 31 problems from a particular rating compared to solving 100s of random problems.
