Napkin Math (thin) — Basic

napkin-01 · Powers of two, orders of magnitude and unit conversions

Questions you must be able to answer aloud:

  • KB/MB/GB/TB/PB in powers of 2 and 10?
  • Seconds in a day (~86,400 ≈ 10^5)?
  • How many requests/day is 1 QPS?
  • Rules of thumb: 1M req/day ≈ 12 QPS.
