ai is somehow eliminating one of the most important things in learning: the process of solving a problem.

the whole point of solving a problem isn't just to get the final answer. it's about the process you go through while trying to solve it.

you start with one problem, get stuck somewhere, explore different approaches, make mistakes, question your logic, and sometimes end up solving another problem along the way.

that's where the actual progress happens.

but with ai, we're slowly moving towards solving the current problem as quickly as possible. we just want the correct answer, the optimized code, or the working solution.

we don't really care about the process anymore.

we're so focused on saving time that we're forgetting what we were supposed to learn in the first place.

the struggle, the debugging, the thinking, the failed approaches, and those random discoveries are all part of becoming a better problem solver.

ai can definitely make us faster, but what's the point of solving 100 problems if we're not developing the ability to think through them ourselves?

Absolute bs thing running in the whole DSA community…
This pattern, that pattern.
Learn these patterns and crack SDE intern at XYZ or whatever…
In my opinion, practice beats pattern-recognition.
Pattern-recognition is more like rote learning:
where you force yourself to remember some predefined template...
The more you actually practice problems, the more it becomes muscle memory...
Think of problem solving like building a graph:
At the very start, you just have a single node.
As you solve more and more problems, the number of nodes increases, connections form, and it slowly turns into a pretty cool graph.
So please stop this pattern BS and just solve more and more problems.
Build crazy-level muscle memory instead.
Thoughts?
