Go — Basic · unit 4/36

Functions: multiple returns, variadics, first-class functions, closures

Questions you must be able to answer aloud:

  • How do multiple return values work and what are named returns?
  • What is a closure? Write a counter generator.
  • What does a variadic function receive inside the body?
  • Is Go pass-by-value or pass-by-reference? (and what about slices/maps?)

Ports, sockets, MAC addresses, ARP, switches vs routers

AI Engineering (LLMs, RAG, agents, inference) — Basic · unit 4/44

Attention and the transformer, at interview level

Questions you must be able to answer aloud:

  • What does self-attention compute (queries, keys, values) and why does it cost O(n^2) in sequence length?
  • Why is the transformer easier to parallelise than an RNN, and what does positional encoding fix?
  • What is multi-head attention, and what is the causal mask in a decoder?
  • Encoder-only vs decoder-only vs encoder-decoder: where do BERT, GPT and T5 sit?
